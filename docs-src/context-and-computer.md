## Open the right column

On a wide window it sits next to the chat, with three tabs: **Computer**, **Routines** and **Context**. On a narrow window, open it from the chat header. The **×** hides it.

## Computer

A small screen, *…'s computer*, shows what the agent is doing: its last steps, or *Asleep* when no session is open. The dot says *working*, *ready* or *waiting for you*.

Below it:

- **In Finder** opens the project, **Processes** lists all running sessions.
- **Working folder**, **Provider**, **Model**, **Access** and **Operate computer**. You can change the model, the access level and computer use right here. **Running for** shows how long the session has been open.
- **This Mac**: **Processor** and **Memory** live, and how warm the Mac runs (*Cool*, *Warm*, *Hot* or *Very hot*, as macOS reports it).
- **Recently edited**: up to four files the agent touched last. Click one to show it in Finder.

## Context

A ring shows what fills the agent's context:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Instructions, tools and skills** | The briefing and everything the program loads at the start. |
| **Conversation** | Your messages and the agent's work so far. |
| **Last answer** | The agent's latest answer. |
| **Free** | What is still left. |

In the middle you see how full it is. Below: **Model**, **Context window**, **From the cache**, the number of **Skills** and **Tools**, and the **5-hour limit** and **Weekly limit** of its plan.

The numbers appear with the agent's first answer.

Below the ring are **Summarise and start fresh** ([Tidying up](tidy-up.md)) and **Manage connectors** ([Connectors](connectors.md)).

## Routines

The middle tab holds the agent's [routines](routines.md).
