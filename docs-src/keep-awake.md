## While agents work

While an agent works, Mochi Squad keeps the Mac from sleeping. The screen may still turn off. When no agent works, the Mac sleeps as usual.

## All the time

In [Settings](settings.md) under **This Mac**, turn on **Keep this Mac awake while Mochi Squad is open**. Then routines and messages from your phone always find it awake.

## What it cannot do

- **A closed MacBook on battery still sleeps.** Plug it in and use an external display, or use a Mac mini.
- **A Mac that is switched off runs nothing.** Routines and messages only work while the Mac is on and awake.
- It works only while Mochi Squad is open. Choose **Keep running in the background** when you quit, and the app stays open, hidden.
