## How it works

Mochi Squad starts the official Claude Code and Codex programs on your Mac. You sign in inside those programs, with your own account. The sign-in stays with the program; Mochi Squad stores no credentials.

## Sign in

1. Click **Sign in** next to Claude Code or Codex. You find the button during setup and in [Settings](settings.md) under **Agent programs**.
2. A window **Sign in to Claude Code** (or **Codex**) opens and your browser opens a moment later.
3. Confirm the sign-in with your subscription in the browser.
4. If the program asks for a code, paste it into the window.

The window closes by itself once the sign-in worked. **Close** cancels it.

## Signing in later

You can skip a program during setup and sign in later. If an agent starts with a program that is not signed in yet, its chat shows a card: *… is waiting for you to sign in*. Click **Open console** and sign in there with your subscription.

## Which plan do I need?

- **Claude Code:** Claude Pro or Max.
- **Codex:** ChatGPT Plus or Pro.

One is enough. Every agent, the coordinator included, can run on either. With Codex you can also use [several ChatGPT plans](chatgpt-plans.md).

Is this allowed? See [Your subscription](subscriptions.md).
