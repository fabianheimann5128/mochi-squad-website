## What Mochi Squad does

Mochi Squad never sees your sign-in. It starts the official, unmodified Claude Code and Codex programs on your own Mac, and you sign in there yourself, with your own account.

## What the providers say

- **Anthropic** states that its rules for third-party apps do not prevent you from signing in to the unmodified Claude Code with your own subscription ([Claude Code legal and compliance](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance)). What third-party apps may not do is handle the sign-in or the tokens themselves, which Mochi Squad does not.
- **OpenAI**'s Codex supports signing in with ChatGPT ([Codex authentication](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/auth)).

## Your part

Your use remains subject to the terms of Anthropic ([Consumer Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms)) and OpenAI ([Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/)), including their usage limits and their rules on automated use.

The plans assume ordinary, individual use. Scheduled routines and many agents in parallel are your own judgement:

- keep them proportionate,
- never share your account with others,
- check the current terms yourself.
