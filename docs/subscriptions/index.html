    Help

    Is this allowed with my subscription?

    What Anthropic and OpenAI say about using your own plan with Mochi Squad, and where the limits of that are.

    #What Mochi Squad does

    Mochi Squad never sees your sign-in. It starts the official, unmodified Claude Code and Codex programs on your own Mac, and you sign in there yourself, with your own account.

    #What the providers say

    • Anthropic states that its rules for third-party apps do not prevent you from signing in to the unmodified Claude Code with your own subscription (Claude Code legal and compliance). What third-party apps may not do is handle the sign-in or the tokens themselves, which Mochi Squad does not.
    • OpenAI's Codex supports signing in with ChatGPT (Codex authentication).

    #Your part

    Your use remains subject to the terms of Anthropic (Consumer Terms) and OpenAI (Terms of Use), including their usage limits and their rules on automated use.

    The plans assume ordinary, individual use. Scheduled routines and many agents in parallel are your own judgement:

    • keep them proportionate,
    • never share your account with others,
    • check the current terms yourself.
    Note

    Mochi Squad is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or OpenAI. This is not legal advice.
