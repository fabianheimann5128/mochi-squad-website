01 — The squad

One is lonely.Hire nine.Or ninety-nine.

Every agent gets a name, a job, a character and a model. Claude Code or Codex, your call. They run on the subscription you already pay for.

02 — Team chat

They talk.You don'tbabysit.

Write once. Your coordinator answers, @name pulls in a specialist, @all wakes everyone. Agents bring in colleagues on their own.

03 — Routines

Night shiftincluded.

Tasks at fixed times through the macOS scheduler, even when the app is closed. Morning report at 07:30. Dependency sweep at 02:00. You sleep.

04 — From your phone

Text yourcoordinatorfrom the bus.

Connect Telegram or WhatsApp. It knows what every agent is doing, hands out work and reports back.

05 — Rights

They play ina sandbox.You holdthe keys.

Read only by default. Edit in project, sandboxed, with your credentials locked away. Full access and computer use only when you flip the switch.

06 — Five promises 1 / 5

Free.Forever.

The app costs nothing. Not now, not later, not for anyone.

Open.

Open source under Apache-2.0. Nothing to hide, so read every line.

Dead simple.

No manual, no prior knowledge. If you need a tutorial, we got it wrong.

Yours stays yours.

Your subscription, your data, your Mac. We run no server and see none of it.

Community first.
